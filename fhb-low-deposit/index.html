First-home buyers with less than 20% deposit

Ticker: fhb-low-deposit • Topic: First-home lending • Status: Verified
Buying a first home without a full 20% deposit is more common than many buyers expect. In September 2026, just over half of all first-home mortgages approved across New Zealand (50.3%—1,342 of 2,667 loans, or $850m in lending) went to buyers with under a 20% deposit, up from 46.0% a year earlier.
