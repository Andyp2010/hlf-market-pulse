Active KiwiSaver provider switching volume

Ticker: kiwisaver-active-switchers • Topic: KiwiSaver scheme transfers • Status: Verified
Reviewing and changing KiwiSaver providers happens more regularly than many members realise—over the year to June 2026, 198,897 New Zealanders moved their balance to a new scheme, averaging 16,575 switches every month.
