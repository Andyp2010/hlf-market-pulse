New Zealand general insurer net loss ratio (net claims incurred ÷ net earned premiums)

Ticker: insurer-loss-ratios • Latest Underlying Data: Annual RBNZ Insurance Series • Last Checked: 10 November 2026 • Creator: Andy Palliser
Also known as: RBNZ general insurer claims loss ratio
Based on Reserve Bank of New Zealand general insurer statistics, licensed New Zealand general insurers recorded an HLF-derived net loss ratio of 63.0% ($4.66B in net incurred claims divided by $7.40B in net earned premiums).
Official dataRBNZ General Insurance Series: Net Incurred Claims = $4.66B; Net Earned Premiums = $7.40B.
HLF calculation($4.66B Net Incurred Claims ÷ $7.40B Net Earned Premiums) × 100 = 63.0%
HLF interpretationHLF market context: A net loss ratio around 60%–68% in non-catastrophe years (and spiking above 80%–100%+ in major weather/earthquake years) shows the proportion of net premiums consumed directly by claims before operating expenses, tax, and RBNZ solvency capital requirements.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • Official published sources: Pulse uses official published data from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), the Financial Markets Authority (FMA), MBIE Tenancy Services, the Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC), and the Accident Compensation Corporation (ACC) wherever available.
  • Unaltered official figures: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures.
  • Explicit derivation labels: Derived statistics (such as percentages, averages, rolling sums, multi-year compound growth rates, and cohort comparisons) are calculated directly from the underlying official series and identified as HLF-derived.
  • Price indices vs. individual quotes: Stats NZ CPI insurance sub-indices measure percentage changes in standardised insurance price indices over time—they do not represent the dollar premium quote for an individual property or policyholder.
  • Reporting lags & differing schedules: Reporting periods differ across agencies (for example, RBNZ lending series publish monthly with a ~4-week lag, MBIE bond data publishes on a ~2-month lag, Stats NZ CPI and IRD student loan balances publish quarterly, and FMA KiwiSaver reports, RBNZ Financial Stability Reports, ACC reports, and regional KiwiSaver breakdowns publish semi-annually or annually).
  • Commitments vs. settlements: RBNZ mortgage lending tables (C31, C32, C33, C40, C41) measure firm bank mortgage commitments/approvals rather than LINZ property title settlements.
  • Publisher revisions: Underlying historical figures may subsequently be revised by the original publisher in later releases.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is unavailable, stale past its freshness window, or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s, saver’s, or policyholder’s credit assessment, underwriting, or lending outcome.
