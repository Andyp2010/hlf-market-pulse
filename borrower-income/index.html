Average gross borrower income for approved first-home buyers vs investors

Ticker: borrower-income • Topic: Borrower income & servicing • Status: Verified
Because mortgage applications often combine two incomes, approved first-home buyers in September 2026 recorded an average gross borrower income of $144.2k nationwide.
