First-home buyers vs property investors monthly lending comparison

Ticker: fhb-vs-investors • Topic: First-home & investor lending • Status: Verified
First-home buyers are out-borrowing property investors across New Zealand. In September 2026, banks approved $1.53B (20.3% of lending) for first-home buyers compared with $1.33B (17.7%) for property investors—putting first-home buyers $195m ahead.
