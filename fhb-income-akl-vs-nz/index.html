Average gross borrower income on approved FHB mortgage applications — New Zealand

Ticker: fhb-income-akl-vs-nz • Latest Underlying Data: September 2026 • Last Checked: 10 November 2026 • Status: verified (current)
Also known as: Approved first-home buyer gross application income by TDTI band (RBNZ C41)
In September 2026, approved New Zealand first-home buyer mortgage applications recorded a borrower-weighted average gross income of $144,232 in RBNZ Table C41—ranging from $197,000 in the lowest Debt-to-Income band (TDTI ≤ 3x) to $123,000 in the highest band (TDTI > 6x).

HLF calculationHarmonic mean across 5 FHB TDTI bands = $144,232 (TDTI ≤ 3x income: $197,000 | 3x < TDTI ≤ 4x income: $151,000 | 4x < TDTI ≤ 5x income: $139,000 | 5x < TDTI ≤ 6x income: $125,000 | TDTI > 6x income: $123,000)
HLF interpretationBecause mortgage applications frequently combine two incomes, the national application average sits higher than single-earner salaries. Buyers on lower or single incomes regularly qualify at lower regional price points or within moderate TDTI bands.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • Official published sources: Pulse uses official published data from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), and MBIE Tenancy Services wherever available.
  • Unaltered official figures: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures.
  • Explicit derivation labels: Derived statistics (such as percentages, averages, rolling sums, and cohort comparisons) are calculated directly from the underlying official series and identified as HLF-derived.
  • Reporting lags & differing schedules: Reporting periods differ across agencies (for example, RBNZ lending series publish monthly with a ~4-week lag, MBIE bond data publishes on a ~2-month lag, CPI and student loan balances publish quarterly, and regional KiwiSaver breakdowns publish annually for the June financial year).
  • Commitments vs. settlements: RBNZ mortgage lending tables (C31, C32, C33, C40, C41) measure firm bank mortgage commitments/approvals rather than LINZ property title settlements.
  • Publisher revisions: Underlying historical figures may subsequently be revised by the original Crown publisher in later releases.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is unavailable, stale past its freshness window, or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual lending outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s credit assessment or lending outcome.

About the HLF Pulse

HLF Pulse is a public reference dataset covering New Zealand mortgage, housing and KiwiSaver statistics. Data is compiled from official releases from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, Stats NZ, Inland Revenue and MBIE Tenancy Services.

Where HLF calculates percentages, averages or comparisons from the underlying official data, these are identified as “Derived by HLF”. The underlying source and relevant reporting period are provided for each statistic.

HLF does not alter or estimate official statistics. Where source data is unavailable, stale or cannot be verified, the relevant series is excluded until it can be confirmed.

Figures are provided for general information and historical market analysis and are not personalised financial advice. Published by HLF (Home Loan Factory) (Public Disclosures • How We Get Paid).
