Total KiwiSaver funds under management (FUM) & official FMA average member balance

Ticker: kiwisaver-total-fum-balance • Observation Period: Year ended 31 March 2026 (Period End: 2026-03-31) • Last Checked: 14 November 2026 • Creator: Andy Palliser
Also known as: FMA KiwiSaver Annual Report total FUM, member count & 10-year nominal/real average balance growth
As at 31 March 2026, total KiwiSaver funds under management reached $138.8B across 3.44 million members, with an average member balance of $40,340—up +213.9% (+12.1%/yr) from $12,852 in 2016 (+134.8%, or +8.9%/yr, after CPI inflation).
Official dataFMA KiwiSaver Annual Report 2026 (year ended 31 March 2026, published 10 September 2026): Official average member balance = $40,340; Total FUM = $138.8B across 3.44m members (vs $12,852 / $33.8B in March 2016).
HLF calculationNominal 10Y: ($40,340 − $12,852) ÷ $12,852 = +213.9% (+12.1%/yr) | Real 10Y (÷1.337 CPI): ($40,340 ÷ $17,183) − 1 = +134.8% (+8.9%/yr)
HLF interpretationHLF market context: Even after accounting for a decade of consumer price inflation, the average KiwiSaver balance has more than doubled in real purchasing-power terms since 2016.

How Dates Are Recorded in HLF Pulse (4-Field Date Standard)

To prevent confusion between when an economic activity occurred and when a government or industry body published its release, every HLF Pulse record separates four distinct date fields:

Date FieldDefinition & How It Is Populated
1. Observation period (observation_period)The period the underlying statistic relates to (for example, September 2026 for monthly RBNZ lending, or Year ended 31 March 2026 for the FMA KiwiSaver Annual Report).
2. Period end (period_end / as_of)The final calendar date covered by the observation period (for example, 2026-09-30 or 2026-03-31). Used by HLF’s automated freshness gate to verify series currency.
3. Source publication date (source_published)When the original organisation released the statistic (for example, the Published Date recorded inside RBNZ’s workbook metadata or on the agency release page). HLF never copies period_end into source_published; where an agency page does not state an exact release date, the agency’s publication schedule is stated explicitly.
4. Last checked (last_checked)The date HLF’s automated GitHub Actions pipeline last fetched and verified the official source.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • 100% live-pulled official sources (zero hardcoded fallbacks): Pulse automatically fetches and parses live data files (.xlsx, .csv, .pdf, and release tables) from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), the Financial Markets Authority (FMA), MBIE Tenancy Services, the Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC), the Accident Compensation Corporation (ACC), and the Financial Services Council (FSC).
  • Unaltered official figures & source rounding: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures. Where official tables publish rounded aggregates (such as RBNZ dollar series rounded to $1m or $0.1m), derived averages and implied counts reflect that source rounding and note it explicitly in the provenance layer.
  • Provenance & derivation layer: To keep the primary editorial summaries natural and readable, exact formulas, series IDs, and derivation labels (Derived by HLF Pulse) are recorded in the expandable provenance layer, Data Dictionary tables, pulse.json, and llms.txt.
  • Price indices vs. individual quotes: Stats NZ CPI insurance sub-indices measure percentage changes in standardised insurance price indices over time—they do not represent the dollar premium quote for an individual property or policyholder.
  • Building consent values vs. turnkey purchase prices: Stats NZ residential building consent values measure the estimated physical construction cost declared on the building consent (excluding land purchase price, developer margin, and off-site subdivision infrastructure).
  • Commitments vs. settlements: RBNZ mortgage lending tables (C31, C32, C33, C40, C41) measure firm bank mortgage commitments/approvals rather than LINZ property title settlements.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is unavailable, stale past its freshness window, or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s, saver’s, or policyholder’s credit assessment, underwriting, or lending outcome.
