Existing owner-occupiers approved with 20% or more equity (<=80% LVR)

Ticker: owner-equity-buffer • Topic: Homeowner equity & refinancing • Status: Verified
Most existing Kiwi homeowners hold more usable equity than they realise. In September 2026, 94.9% of existing owner-occupier mortgages approved across New Zealand (14,327 of 15,096 loans, or $4.16B) had at least 20% equity, carrying an average loan size of $290,082.
