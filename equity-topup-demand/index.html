Total monthly dollar equity unlocked via mortgage top-ups

Ticker: equity-topup-demand • Topic: Homeowner equity & top-ups • Status: Verified
Tapping into home equity without selling is one of the most common moves in New Zealand banking—in September 2026, homeowners unlocked $962m in mortgage top-ups across 9,639 existing properties (averaging $100k per top-up) for renovations, debt consolidation, and investment deposits.
