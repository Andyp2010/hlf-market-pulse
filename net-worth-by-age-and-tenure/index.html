New Zealand household net worth by housing tenure: owner-occupied vs non-owner-occupied (Stats NZ)

In Stats NZ's triennial Household Net Worth statistics (year ended June 2024), median household net worth across all New Zealand households was $529,000—with households in owner-occupied dwellings recording a median net worth of $989,000 ($1,287,000 for mortgage-free homeowners and $727,000 for mortgaged homeowners) compared with $57,000 for households in non-owner-occupied dwellings (a ratio of 17.4x).

Definition & Methodology

Definition: Median household net worth (total assets minus total liabilities) across all New Zealand households, households in owner-occupied dwellings, and households in non-owner-occupied dwellings from Stats NZ's Household Net Worth statistics.

Methodology: Extracted live from Stats NZ's triennial Household Net Worth statistics release (year ended June 2024). Compares median household net worth across all New Zealand households, all owner-occupied dwellings (split into mortgaged and mortgage-free households), and non-owner-occupied dwellings.
