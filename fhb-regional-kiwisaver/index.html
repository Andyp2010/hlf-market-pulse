Regional KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal averages across New Zealand

Ticker: fhb-regional-kiwisaver • Topic: KiwiSaver & regional first-home deposits • Status: Verified
Pooling two KiwiSaver accounts is how most Kiwi couples build their deposit—over the year to June 2026, 50,330 first-home buyers withdrew $2.27B from KiwiSaver, averaging $45,005 per person nationwide (~$90,000 per couple), led by Wellington ($50,364/person) and Auckland ($47,630/person).
