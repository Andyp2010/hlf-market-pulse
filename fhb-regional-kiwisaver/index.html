Regional KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal averages per member across New Zealand

Ticker: fhb-regional-kiwisaver • Latest Underlying Data: Year ended June 2026 • Last Checked: 10 November 2026 • Creator: Andy Palliser
Also known as: KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal average by NZ region (16 regions)
Over the year ended 30 June 2026, Inland Revenue recorded 50,330 KiwiSaver members withdrawing $2.27B for a first home across New Zealand's 16 regions—an HLF-derived mean of $45,005 per withdrawing member nationwide, ranging from $50,364/member in Wellington (and $47,630/member in Auckland) to $39,916/member in Southland.
Official dataInland Revenue Regional KiwiSaver Withdrawals (Year ended 30 June 2026): 50,330 members withdrew $2,265,097,300 across 16 NZ regions.
HLF calculation$2,265,097,300 ÷ 50,330 members = $45,005/member (Regional range: $39,916–$50,364)
HLF interpretationHLF market context: IRD reports withdrawals per individual member (not per couple). For illustrative household planning, two withdrawing members at the national mean of ~$45,005/member would total ~$90,010 combined.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • Official published sources: Pulse uses official published data from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), and MBIE Tenancy Services wherever available.
  • Unaltered official figures: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures.
  • Explicit derivation labels: Derived statistics (such as percentages, averages, rolling sums, and cohort comparisons) are calculated directly from the underlying official series and identified as HLF-derived.
  • Reporting lags & differing schedules: Reporting periods differ across agencies (for example, RBNZ lending series publish monthly with a ~4-week lag, MBIE bond data publishes on a ~2-month lag, CPI and student loan balances publish quarterly, and regional KiwiSaver breakdowns publish annually for the June financial year).
  • Commitments vs. settlements: RBNZ mortgage lending tables (C31, C32, C33, C40, C41) measure firm bank mortgage commitments/approvals rather than LINZ property title settlements.
  • Publisher revisions: Underlying historical figures may subsequently be revised by the original Crown publisher in later releases.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is unavailable, stale past its freshness window, or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual lending outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s credit assessment or lending outcome.

About the HLF Pulse & Creator

HLF Pulse is a public reference dataset covering New Zealand mortgage, housing and KiwiSaver statistics, published by HLF (Home Loan Factory) and created by Andy Palliser (Andrew Palliser), Mortgage & KiwiSaver Adviser at Home Loan Factory.

Data is compiled from official releases from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, Stats NZ, Inland Revenue and MBIE Tenancy Services. Where HLF calculates percentages, averages or comparisons from the underlying official data, these are identified as “Derived by HLF”. The underlying source and relevant reporting period are provided for each statistic.

HLF does not alter or estimate official statistics. Where source data is unavailable, stale or cannot be verified, the relevant series is excluded until it can be confirmed.

Figures are provided for general information and historical market analysis and are not personalised financial advice. Published by HLF (Home Loan Factory) (Creator Profile: Andy Palliser • Public Disclosures • How We Get Paid).
