New Zealand national and regional median weekly private rent

Ticker: rent-yield-pulse • Topic: Rents & property investment yields • Status: Verified
Private weekly rents across New Zealand have been relatively steady over the past year—the national median sat at $595/wk in August 2026 (up 0.8% on a year earlier), with regional medians at $650/wk in Auckland, $580/wk in Wellington, and $550/wk in Canterbury.
