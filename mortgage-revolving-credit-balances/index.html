Residential revolving credit drawn mortgage balances & share of housing lending (RBNZ Table S32)

Ticker: mortgage-revolving-credit-balances • Observation Period: September 2026 (Period End: 2026-09-30) • Last Checked: 16 November 2026 • Creator: Andy Palliser
Also known as: RBNZ Table S32 Banks: Assets – Loans by Product — Owner-occupied & investor revolving credit balances ($B)
As at September 2026, New Zealand borrowers held $7.00B ($7,005m) in drawn residential revolving credit mortgage balances (representing 1.8% of all bank residential mortgage lending—comprising $5.74B [1.9%] across owner-occupiers and $1.27B [1.2%] across residential property investors) (down 3.9% year-on-year from $7.29B a year earlier).
Official dataRBNZ Table S32 (September 2026): Registered bank residential revolving credit drawn balances = $7,005m ($7.00B).
HLF calculationTotal Residential Revolving Credit (September 2026): $5,737m (Owner-Occupier) + $1,268m (Investor) = $7,005m ($7.00B) | Share of Housing Loans: $7,005m ÷ $399,563m = 1.8% (OO: 1.9%, Investor: 1.2%)
HLF interpretationHLF market context: Revolving credit facilities act as an all-in-one transactional mortgage slice where incoming salary and cash buffers reduce the daily balance on which interest is calculated.

How Dates Are Recorded in HLF Pulse (4-Field Date Standard)

To prevent confusion between when an economic activity occurred and when a government or industry body published its release, every HLF Pulse record separates four distinct date fields:

Date FieldDefinition & How It Is Populated
1. Observation period (observation_period)The period the underlying statistic relates to (for example, September 2026 for monthly RBNZ lending, or Year ended 31 March 2026 for the FMA KiwiSaver Annual Report).
2. Period end (period_end / as_of)The final calendar date covered by the observation period (for example, 2026-09-30 or 2026-03-31). Used by HLF’s automated freshness gate to verify series currency.
3. Source publication date (source_published)When the original organisation released the statistic. HLF never copies period_end into source_published; where an agency page does not state an exact release date, the agency’s publication schedule is stated explicitly.
4. Last checked (last_checked)The date HLF’s automated GitHub Actions pipeline last fetched and verified the official source.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • 100% live-pulled official sources (zero hardcoded fallbacks): Pulse automatically fetches and parses live data files (.xlsx, .csv, .pdf, and release tables) from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), the Financial Markets Authority (FMA), MBIE Tenancy Services, Kāinga Ora, the Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC), and the Accident Compensation Corporation (ACC).
  • Unaltered official figures & source rounding: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures. Where official tables publish rounded aggregates (such as RBNZ dollar series rounded to $1m or $0.1m), derived averages and implied counts reflect that source rounding and note it explicitly in the provenance layer.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is stale past its freshness window or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s, saver’s, or policyholder’s credit assessment, underwriting, or lending outcome.
