First-home buyer share of total New Zealand mortgage lending

Ticker: fhb-share • Topic: First-home lending • Status: Verified
First-home buyers continue to make up an active slice of the New Zealand market—in September 2026, banks approved 2,667 first-home mortgages (20.3% of all new mortgage lending, up from 19.2% a year earlier), with an average loan size of $574k.
