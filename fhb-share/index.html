First-home buyer share of total New Zealand mortgage lending

Ticker: fhb-share • Latest Underlying Data: September 2026 • Last Checked: 10 November 2026 • Creator: Andy Palliser
Also known as: FHB market share of residential mortgage lending
In September 2026, registered banks approved 2,667 first-home buyer mortgages totalling $1.53B—representing an HLF-derived 20.3% share of all new residential mortgage lending (up from 19.2% a year earlier) and a mean loan size of $574k.
Official dataRBNZ Table C31 (September 2026): 2,667 FHB borrowers and $1,530m FHB lending out of $7,533m total new residential lending.
HLF calculation$1,530m ÷ $7,533m = 20.3% (Avg loan: $1,530m ÷ 2,667 = $574k)
HLF interpretationHLF market context: Tracks the proportion of new bank mortgage commitments going to first-home buyers relative to other owner-occupiers, investors, and business borrowers.

Data Quality & Limitations

  • Official published sources: Pulse uses official published data from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, Inland Revenue (IRD), MBIE Tenancy Services, the Insurance Council of New Zealand (ICNZ), and the Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC) wherever available.
  • Unaltered official figures: HLF does not alter or estimate official source figures.
  • Explicit derivation labels: Derived statistics (such as percentages, averages, rolling sums, and cohort comparisons) are calculated directly from the underlying official series and identified as HLF-derived.
  • Price indices vs. individual quotes: Stats NZ CPI insurance sub-indices measure percentage changes in standardised insurance price indices over time—they do not represent the dollar premium quote for an individual property or policyholder.
  • Reporting lags & differing schedules: Reporting periods differ across agencies (for example, RBNZ lending series publish monthly with a ~4-week lag, MBIE bond data publishes on a ~2-month lag, Stats NZ CPI and IRD student loan balances publish quarterly, and RBNZ Financial Stability Reports, ICNZ market data, and regional KiwiSaver breakdowns publish semi-annually or annually).
  • Commitments vs. settlements: RBNZ mortgage lending tables (C31, C32, C33, C40, C41) measure firm bank mortgage commitments/approvals rather than LINZ property title settlements.
  • Publisher revisions: Underlying historical figures may subsequently be revised by the original publisher in later releases.
  • Automatic staleness exclusion: HLF automatically excludes any series where the underlying source is unavailable, stale past its freshness window, or cannot be verified.
  • Market context vs. individual outcomes: Statistics describe aggregate historical market cohorts and do not predict an individual borrower’s or policyholder’s credit assessment, underwriting, or lending outcome.

About the HLF Pulse & Creator

HLF Pulse is a public reference dataset covering New Zealand mortgage, housing, KiwiSaver, and protection statistics, published by HLF (Home Loan Factory) (HLF Financial Limited (FSP1008571)) and created by Andy Palliser (Andrew Jonathan Palliser, FSP1004725), Mortgage & KiwiSaver Adviser at Home Loan Factory.

Data is compiled from official releases from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, Stats NZ, Inland Revenue, MBIE Tenancy Services, the Insurance Council of New Zealand, and the Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake. Where HLF calculates percentages, averages, or comparisons from the underlying official data, these are identified as “Derived by HLF”.

HLF does not alter or estimate official statistics. Where source data is unavailable, stale, or cannot be verified, the relevant series is excluded until it can be confirmed.
