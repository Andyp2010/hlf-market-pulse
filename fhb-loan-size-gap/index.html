First-home buyer average mortgage size by deposit tier (<20% vs 20%+ deposit)

Ticker: fhb-loan-size-gap • Topic: First-home lending • Status: Verified
First-home buyers with under a 20% deposit actually take out larger mortgages on average ($633,383) than buyers with a 20%+ deposit ($513,208)—a $120,175 difference in September 2026, as higher-earning couples often use a 10%–15% deposit to buy sooner rather than renting for extra years to save 20%.
