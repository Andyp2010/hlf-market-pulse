Property investor equity tier breakdown (40%+, 30%–40%, and <30% equity)

Ticker: investor-equity-tiers • Topic: Property investment & equity • Status: Verified
You don't need 40% equity locked up to buy an investment property. In September 2026, 43.8% of approved NZ property investors had 40%+ equity—while 36.4% bought in the 30%–40% equity band and 19.8% (527 loans, or $337m) were approved with under 30% equity.
