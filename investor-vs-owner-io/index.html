Investor vs owner-occupier interest-only mortgage share

Ticker: investor-vs-owner-io • Topic: Loan structure & interest-only • Status: Verified
Loan structures look quite different between rental properties and family homes in the Reserve Bank figures—in September 2026, 31.5% of new investor lending was set up on interest-only terms (including revolving credit), compared with 13.7% for owner-occupiers.
