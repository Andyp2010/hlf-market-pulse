Property investors approved with under a 30% deposit (>70% LVR new-build & speed-limit tier)

Ticker: investor-new-build-lvr • Topic: Property investment & new builds • Status: Verified
Because brand-new builds are exempt from Reserve Bank LVR restrictions, nearly one in five property investors buys with less than the standard 30% existing-property deposit. In September 2026, 527 investor loans ($337m, or 19.8% of investor approvals) were approved with under a 30% deposit (>70% LVR)—comprising 475 loans ($301m) in the 20%–30% deposit band (which includes 20%-deposit new builds) and 52 loans ($36m) with under a 20% deposit (>80% LVR).
