Weighted-average settled 1-year and 2-year fixed mortgage interest rates

Ticker: settled-rates • Topic: Mortgage interest rates • Status: Verified
Advertised bank rates only tell half the story—across all new fixed mortgages actually settled in September 2026, New Zealand borrowers locked in a weighted-average rate of 4.82% for one year and 5.09% for two years.
