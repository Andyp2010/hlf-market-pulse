Monthly KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal count, value & average per buyer

Ticker: kiwisaver-first-home • Topic: KiwiSaver & first-home deposits • Status: Verified
For many buyers, KiwiSaver does much of the heavy lifting toward a first deposit—in September 2026, 4,250 members withdrew a combined $195.7m to put toward a first home—averaging ~$46k per withdrawing member, or roughly $73k of KiwiSaver funds per approved first-home mortgage.
