Annual new residential dwellings consented in New Zealand

Ticker: new-builds-annual • Topic: New builds & housing supply • Status: Verified
New-build supply across New Zealand has been gathering pace, with 41,091 new homes consented in the year to September 2026 (up 18.0% on the previous year, including 3,570 dwellings consented in September 2026).
