New Zealand median student loan balance

Ticker: student-loan-servicing • Topic: Borrowing power & student loans • Status: Verified
Student debt is a familiar line item on home loan applications—across Inland Revenue's June 2026 figures, the median NZ student loan balance sat at $18,301 (up 4.5% on a year earlier), where compulsory PAYE deductions factor into how banks test monthly servicing.
