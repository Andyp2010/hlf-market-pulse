Annual rolling 12-month first-home buyer mortgage approvals

Ticker: fhb-annual-milestone • Topic: First-home lending • Status: Verified
Over the 12 months to September 2026, New Zealand banks approved 33,485 first-home buyer mortgages totalling $19.61B in lending (up from 31,589 loans a year earlier)—including 15,743 buyers (47.0%) who bought with under a 20% deposit.
