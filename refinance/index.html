Monthly mortgage refinancing ( borrowers switching bank providers)

Ticker: refinance • Topic: Refinancing & bank switching • Status: Verified
Moving banks is a regular part of the New Zealand mortgage market rather than a rare step—in September 2026 alone, 2,772 borrowers switched mortgage providers, moving $1.73B in loans (23.0% of all new lending, at an average loan size of $624k).
