Annual residential construction cost inflation (CPI purchase of newly built home)

Ticker: build-cost-inflation • Topic: New builds & construction costs • Status: Verified
Residential build-cost inflation has settled into single digits compared with the post-pandemic construction surge—Stats NZ recorded a +3.2% annual change in the cost of purchasing a newly built home in the September 2026 quarter, alongside 3.7% overall CPI inflation.
