Core Definitions & Classification Standards

First-Home Buyer (FHB) Lending (RBNZ C31)Firm mortgage commitments issued by registered NZ banks to borrowers purchasing a residential property to occupy as their first home. Measures bank loan commitments rather than LINZ title settlements.
Low-Deposit / High-LVR Lending (>80% LVR)Mortgages where the loan amount exceeds 80.0% of the property's value (meaning the buyer's deposit or equity is strictly under 20.0%). Loans with an exact 20.0% deposit (80.0% LVR) are classified by RBNZ inside ≤80% LVR.
Property Investor Lending & LVR Tiers (RBNZ C31)Lending secured against residential investment property. RBNZ tracks investor LVR tiers at ≤60% LVR (40%+ equity), 60%–70% LVR (30%–40% equity), 70%–80% LVR (20%–30% equity), and >80% LVR (<20% equity).
Debt-to-Income (TDTI) Ratio (RBNZ C40)Total borrower debt (including existing mortgages, student loans, and credit limits) divided by annual gross before-tax borrower income. RBNZ's DTI speed limit allows banks up to 20% of new FHB lending above 6x DTI (new builds are exempt).
Borrower Gross Income — BGI (RBNZ C41)Total pre-tax annual gross income reported on approved mortgage applications (combining single and joint earners, plus eligible boarder/rental income). HLF computes the exact borrower-weighted harmonic mean across all TDTI bands.
Settled Mortgage Rates vs. Advertised Rates (RBNZ B30)RBNZ Table B30 records the dollar-weighted average interest rate across mortgages actually settled during the month, capturing negotiated and unadvertised bank rate discounts rather than carded rack rates.

About the HLF Pulse

HLF Pulse is a public reference dataset covering New Zealand mortgage, housing and KiwiSaver statistics. Data is compiled from official releases from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, Stats NZ, Inland Revenue and MBIE Tenancy Services.

Where HLF calculates percentages, averages or comparisons from the underlying official data, these are identified as “Derived by HLF”. The underlying source and relevant reporting period are provided for each statistic.
