Methodology, 3-Layer Provenance & 4-Field Date Standard

  • Three-Layer Separation: Every ticker explicitly separates Official Data (as reported by RBNZ, Stats NZ, Inland Revenue, MBIE Tenancy Services, HUD / MCERT, FMA, Kāinga Ora, NHC, or ACC), HLF Calculation (exact arithmetic formula and inputs), and HLF Interpretation (scope notes and market context).
  • Four-Field Date Architecture: Every record tracks (1) observation_period (the period the underlying data covers, ranging from triennial and annual surveys to monthly series), (2) period_end (ISO YYYY-MM-DD end date), (3) source_published (when the source agency released the data, or its release schedule where no timestamp is embedded in the source file), and (4) last_checked (when HLF Pulse last verified the release).
  • Automated Validation Guardrails: Records are checked for composition sum-to-100 consistency, demographic cohort reconciliation, and unit scaling before publication.
