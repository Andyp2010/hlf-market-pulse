What is the HLF Pulse?

The HLF Pulse is a public reference dataset covering New Zealand mortgage, housing, and KiwiSaver statistics. It brings together selected statistics from official New Zealand Crown sources including the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ), Stats NZ, MBIE Tenancy Services, and Inland Revenue (IRD).

HLF does not create the underlying official statistics. We collect, standardise, and, where relevant, derive selected measures from those published datasets so they can be used consistently across HLF articles, interactive calculators, and educational resources.

Each statistic identifies its underlying official source, reporting period, methodology, historical values, and whether the figure is directly reported or derived by HLF. The dataset is designed to make New Zealand mortgage and housing data easier for people, researchers, publishers, and information systems to find, understand, and verify.

1. Officially reportedFigures published directly by the Crown agency (e.g., loan counts, dollar lending totals, settled rates in RBNZ B30, building consents, median rents).
2. HLF-derivedProportions, shares, and per-borrower averages calculated by HLF from two official series (e.g., LVRN.MMB2.BC1 ÷ LVRN.MMB2.AC1).
3. HLF-calculated comparisonYear-on-year percentage point changes, 12-month rolling totals, cohort gaps, and headroom against RBNZ speed limits.
4. HLF interpretationPlain-English educational context explaining how lending rules, bank test rates, or exemptions apply in practice.

Explicit Definitions & Official Methodology Reference

First-Home Buyer (FHB) Lending (RBNZ C31)Firm mortgage commitments issued by registered NZ banks to borrowers purchasing a residential property to occupy as their first home. Measures bank loan commitments rather than LINZ title settlements.
Low-Deposit / High-LVR Lending (>80% LVR)Mortgages where the loan amount exceeds 80.0% of the property's value (meaning the buyer's deposit or equity is strictly under 20.0%). Loans with an exact 20.0% deposit (80.0% LVR) are classified by RBNZ inside ≤80% LVR.
Property Investor Lending & LVR Tiers (RBNZ C31)Lending secured against residential investment property. RBNZ tracks investor LVR tiers at ≤60% LVR (40%+ equity), 60%–70% LVR (30%–40% equity), 70%–80% LVR (20%–30% equity, including 20%-deposit new builds), and >80% LVR (<20% equity).
Debt-to-Income (TDTI) Ratio (RBNZ C40)Total borrower debt (including existing mortgages, student loans, and credit limits) divided by annual gross before-tax borrower income. RBNZ's DTI speed limit allows banks up to 20% of new FHB lending above 6x DTI (new builds are exempt).
Borrower Gross Income — BGI (RBNZ C41)Total pre-tax annual gross income reported on approved mortgage applications (combining single and joint earners, plus eligible boarder/rental income). HLF computes the exact borrower-weighted harmonic mean across all TDTI bands.
Settled Mortgage Rates vs. Advertised Rates (RBNZ B30)RBNZ Table B30 records the dollar-weighted average interest rate across mortgages actually settled during the month, capturing negotiated and unadvertised bank rate discounts rather than carded rack rates.
