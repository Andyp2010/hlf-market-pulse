Property investor loans approved with under 20% deposit (>80% LVR) & 20%–30% deposit

Ticker: investor-low-deposit • Topic: Property investment & new builds • Status: Verified
Even for property investors, low-deposit approvals happen every month through new-build exemptions and bank speed limits. In September 2026, New Zealand banks approved 52 investor mortgages ($36m, or 1.9% of investors) with strictly under a 20% deposit (>80% LVR)—up from 34 loans ($23m) a year earlier (and 508 loans / $328m over the past 12 months)—plus another 475 investor loans ($301m, or 17.8%) in the 20%–30% deposit tier (70%–80% LVR) where standard 20%-deposit new-build investment loans sit.
