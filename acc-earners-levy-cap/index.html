ACC Earners' Levy rate, Maximum Liable Earnings cap & 80% weekly compensation ceiling

For the 2026/27 tax year, the ACC Earners' Levy is $1.75 per $100 (1.75% incl. GST) up to a Maximum Liable Earnings cap of $156,641 (a maximum annual levy of $2,741.22), capping ACC's 80% weekly compensation at $125,312.80/year ($2,409.86/week gross).
